01 · Context

Operational evidence was fragmented across specialist systems.

The goal was not to replace Grafana, Prometheus or security scanners. It was to connect their evidence to ownership and action.

Metrics, logs, traces, deployments, vulnerabilities and recovery evidence often live in separate tools. Engineers still need a coherent answer to four questions: what is affected, who owns it, what changed, and what should happen next?

I designed ObserveX as a multi-workspace React application backed by a separate GitOps platform repository. The application provides focused operational workflows while established open-source systems remain the sources of telemetry truth.
