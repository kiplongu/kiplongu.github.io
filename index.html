OBSERVEX

Application + platform engineering

One operational command centre. Clear ownership from code to recovery.

ObserveX is a multi-workspace observability and DevSecOps product backed by a separate GitOps platform repository. I designed and implemented its application architecture, identity, delivery controls, telemetry integrations, reliability workflows and recovery path.

DeliveryGitOps authorityArgoCD reconciliation

The challenge

Operational truth was fragmented across delivery, telemetry, security and recovery systems.

Specialist tools remain excellent sources of truth, but engineers still need one place to understand service ownership, incidents, SLOs, security posture and deployment risk.

ObserveX connects that context without pretending to replace Prometheus, Grafana, Loki, Tempo, ArgoCD or security scanners.

SRE · four golden signals

Traffic, errors, latency and saturation become actionable service context.

ErrorsError ratio
LatencyP95 / P99
SaturationCapacity

Prometheus rules calculate indicators and multi-window burn rates. Alertmanager sends authenticated firing and resolved events into ObserveX incidents, preserving service and runbook context.
